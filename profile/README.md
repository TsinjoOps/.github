## What we're building

**TsinjoOps** is an emerging technology initiative focused on making dependable digital systems more accessible to organizations.

We bring together open-source applications, practical engineering and carefully governed AI automation—so teams can spend less time managing technical complexity and more time on their work.

### Our areas of focus

| Enterprise applications | Engineering & operations |
| :--- | :--- |
| Business workflows and integrations built around technologies such as **Frappe / ERPNext**, with identity and access management where needed. | Repeatable deployments, monitoring, incident investigation, code reviews and **human-approved** changes. |
| **Data & applied AI** | **Digital presence** |
| Dashboards, data integration and optional private AI assistants, using fit-for-purpose open-source components. | Accessible websites, technical SEO, content workflows and evidence-based AI-search visibility analysis (GEO). |

### How we approach technology

- **Adopt before reinventing.** Prefer proven open-source building blocks, then configure, integrate and extend.
- **Keep people in control.** AI can assist diagnosis, coding and drafting; consequential actions require appropriate authorization and review.
- **Design for continuity.** Make deployments reproducible, access revocable, data portable and operations auditable.
- **Measure outcomes.** Treat reliability, security, performance and search visibility as things to verify—not promises generated by a dashboard.

### Technology we're exploring

**Applications:** Frappe / ERPNext · Authentik · Apache Superset · LibreChat  
**Engineering & operations:** Hermes Agent · GitHub Actions · observability tooling  
**Data:** PostgreSQL · dbt · dlt  
**Web & visibility:** Astro · OpenSEO · GEO Optimizer · Elmo

These are areas of evaluation and integration, **not** a claim that every component is deployed, offered commercially or appropriate for every organization.

### Our identity

Our symbol combines a structured **T** with the colors of **air, fire, water and earth**—perspective, energy, adaptability and stability. The visual language aims to balance technological precision with a human, approachable character.
