# TsinjoOps — visual identity

**Language / Langue :** English · [Français](https://github.com/TsinjoOps/.github/blob/main/profile/BRAND.fr.md)


> **Status:** V3 “Elemental Focus” — current visual direction; distinctiveness, recall, and trademark clearance still require validation.

The logo is a central geometric **T** framed by four open corners representing the forces of nature. Keep it simple, legible and recognizable across interfaces, documents, and web surfaces.

## Color palette

| **Air** | `#9BC9CF` | Perspective, anticipation |
| **Fire** | `#F07B5F` | Energy, action |
| **Water** | `#2789A0` | Adaptability, flow |
| **Earth** | `#B78F6D` | Stability, dependability |
| **Canvas** | `#F5F8F7` | Open, clean background |
| **Accessible Water** | `#176779` | Darker accent for small white-on-blue UI text |

Use Night as the visual foundation and the four elemental colors **as accents rather than competing primaries**. Do not use the lighter accent colors for small text without independently checking contrast.

## Typography

- **Sora** — display headings and primary logotype.
- **Inter** — interface text, documentation and long-form reading.
- Use sensible system-font fallbacks where these typefaces are unavailable.

## Logo usage

- Use the **full four-element mark** when it has adequate space (approximately 64 px or larger).
- At favicon scale (16–48 px), use the simplified monochrome micro-T from the V3 Penpot file; do not compress the detailed full-color icon into tiny formats.
- Choose the light or dark lockup to preserve legibility.
- Keep clear space around the symbol and preserve its aspect ratio.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows or rearrange the element colors.
- Do not imply that the logo, brand name or artwork has completed legal clearance.

## Brand voice

**Precise, approachable, transparent.** Explain trade-offs clearly. Say what has been verified and what remains experimental. Never imply client endorsements, capabilities or performance guarantees without evidence.

**Working signature:** *Anticipate. Build. Operate.*
